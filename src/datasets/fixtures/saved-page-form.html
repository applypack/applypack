Senior Backend Engineer

Fennel Works · Lisbon or remote within the EU

About the role

You will own the payments ledger, its APIs and its on-call rota, working with two product teams across three time zones. The ledger moves forty million euros a month and has not lost a cent in four years; keeping it that way is the job.

What we ask

  • Five years with Node.js and PostgreSQL in production
  • Experience with double-entry accounting or payments
  • Care for the people who read your code after you

What we offer

€85,000–€105,000, 30 days of leave, and a team that reviews every change within a day.
